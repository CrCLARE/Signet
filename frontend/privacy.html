1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Example Company ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use the platform at this website (the "Service").

We are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and transparently. Please read this policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

Data Purpose Required
Email address Account registration and sign-in Yes
Display name (nickname) Shown on the public license lookup page Yes
Password (hashed) Account authentication — stored as a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash, never in plaintext Yes
Order number To bind a purchase to your account Only when binding an order
Product names To issue and manage licenses for your products Only when creating products

2.2 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your payment card details, bank account information, or any encryption keys used by your products. Payment processing is handled entirely by the third-party payment provider you choose.

2.3 Automatically collected technical data

When you make a request to the Service, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may log limited technical information such as the request timestamp and general geographic region of the request. This data is used only for security, abuse prevention, and service reliability.

3. How we use your data

We use the personal data we collect strictly to:

  • Provide the Service, including account creation, sign-in, and order binding;
  • Issue licenses that you request;
  • Display your public display name and product names on the license lookup page;
  • Respond to your support requests, security reports, or refund inquiries;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. Cookies and local storage

4.1 Local storage (essential)

The Service stores your sign-in session locally in your browser using localStorage. This is required for the Service to work — without it, you would need to sign in on every page.

Key Purpose Lifetime
cs_auth_token Your sign-in session (JWT) Until expiry or sign-out
cs_auth_user Cached display name and email for UI Until sign-out
cs_admin_token Administrator session (only on /admin.html) Until expiry or sign-out

4.2 Analytics (optional)

This deployment does not use any third-party analytics. This deployment uses a third-party analytics service to understand aggregate traffic patterns. The analytics service may set cookies to distinguish unique visitors. You can opt out via your browser's tracking protection settings or a browser extension.

    6. Data storage and security

    Your data is stored in Cloudflare's D1 database, distributed globally across Cloudflare's network.

    • All communication between your browser and the Service is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.
    • Passwords are stored as PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes with per-user salts; they are never stored or transmitted in plaintext.
    • Administrator credentials are stored in encrypted environment variables, not in the database.
    • Administrative actions are recorded in an audit log for accountability.

    While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security against all threats.

    Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you;
    • Correct inaccurate data;
    • Delete your account and personal data;
    • Export a machine-readable copy of your data;
    • Object to or restrict certain processing;
    • Withdraw consent at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our support address. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

    Deleting your account does not delete licenses already issued and in the possession of your customers. Those licenses remain valid unless separately revoked for cause, because they are cryptographically signed offline and independent of your account.

    8. Children's privacy
