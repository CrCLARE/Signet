# Sponsors

Signet is free and open source. It's maintained by a small team on their own
time. If it saves you hours of work, consider supporting the project.

## Current sponsors

The full list of supporters is maintained at:

**[crclare.top/reward](https://crclare.top/reward)**

Every contribution — the cost of a coffee, a domain renewal, a small donation —
directly funds the ongoing development of Signet and other CrCLARE Studio
projects.

## Become a sponsor

Sponsorship is handled through the CrCLARE Studio reward page. Any amount is
welcome, and recurring support is especially appreciated.

**→ [Sponsor Signet](https://crclare.top/reward)**

### What your support goes toward

- Domain and DNS costs
- Testing infrastructure
- Software licenses for design tools
- The maintainer's time

### Recognition

By default, sponsors are listed on the reward page with their display name and
chosen amount. If you'd prefer to stay anonymous, or would like your listing
adjusted, contact `hi@crclare.top`.
